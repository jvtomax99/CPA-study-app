**Working on now (2026-11-03): smoother full-screen 3D navigation. Not committed yet.** Jose found moving around the full-screen city laggy, the drag and pinch off, the camera fighting back and the street hard to reach.
- Causes found: the camera jumped to a roof plus 12 m the moment it touched a building's box (and the map's buildings were boxes around their outlines); zoom stopped 45 m out and the camera never went below 20 m; a drag pinned to the y = 0 plane flung the city near the horizon; any twist in a pinch turned the view; the edges were hard walls; every pointer event ran hit tests.
- Now (in town.js, the full-screen section): `CAM` is where the camera wants to be and `look()` stands it just in front of any building it ends up inside, easing (in quickly, out over about 0.3 s), never snapping. Low down, `moveBy` slides along walls. Solids keep the map's real outlines (`inRing`). Zoom goes to 4 m from the pivot and no closer than 5 m to the point zoomed at; `phiMax(r)` lets the view tilt to the horizon as it closes in and `pivotY(r)` lifts the pivot to eye height. A drag pins to the height of whatever is under the finger and each step is capped by the camera's height; above the horizon it moves by screen distance. Pinch zooms after a 6% spread and turns after 25 px around the finger circle (MapLibre's threshold). Past the city's edge moves go stiff and spring back. Glide decays at 0.92 per 60 Hz frame at any frame rate.
- Pointer moves are recorded and applied once per frame (`pointers()` from `fsUpdate`). Double-tap zooms in (a single tap waits 260 ms to tell), a two-finger tap zooms out, the wheel zooms (a trackpad pinch too), keys move (arrows or WASD, Q and E turn, R and F tilt, + and -). If frames run over 22 ms while moving, it draws at 1x pixels until the view stops (`moveRes`). Crowd walkers' plaza stretch is worked out once instead of every frame.
- New buttons: the compass (turns with the view, tap to face north) and Street (eye level on the red steps, looking south to One Times Square). `__townDebug().pose` reports the camera for tests.

**Fixed: full-screen 3D black below a line on iPhone** (reported 2026-11-02, after the NaN fix below: only the top strip of the full-screen city drew, the rest black, labels fine). **Fixed: town sometimes going black on iPhone** (reported 2026-11-02 with a screenshot: labels drawn, 3D area pure black). Reproduced by injecting one NaN: the bloom blur smears a single NaN pixel over the whole frame. Cause: `pow(vL.y, 2.2)` in the wet-street reflection shader, where vL.y can land a hair below zero at a quad edge and Apple GPUs return NaN; now clamped. Guard: a pass before the bloom zeroes any NaN or infinite pixel. Also: if iOS drops the WebGL context the town redraws when it comes back, and otherwise shows "Restart 3D" instead of a black box; MSAA is 2x on touch devices. `__townDebug().nanTest()` re-runs the injection; the `?fps=1` meter shows a lost-context count.
