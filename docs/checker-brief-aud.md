# Brief for independently fact-checking new CPA AUD questions

You did not write these questions. Check them as a skeptical reviewer. The bank is the main study source for an accountant preparing for the CPA exam's Auditing and Attestation (AUD) section, so a wrong key teaches a wrong rule.

Scope: the AUD section of the AICPA's 2026 blueprint (/home/claude/aud-blueprint.txt) and only its listed References. Nonissuer audits follow the AICPA standards (AU-C), issuer audits follow the PCAOB standards (AS), and attestation, review and compilation engagements follow AT-C and AR-C. The bank keys the current codified text. Where a standard took effect for periods or engagements beginning on or after December 15, 2025 (SAS No. 146, SSAE No. 23), the key must also hold under the earlier wording; SAS No. 149, 150 and 151 are not yet effective and must not change any key.

For EVERY question in your assigned file:
1. Is the keyed answer ("answer" is a 0-based index into "choices") correct under the standard that applies to the entity and engagement in the stem? Recompute every calculation.
2. Is exactly one choice defensibly correct? Flag any question where a careful candidate could defend a second choice, or where the stem leaves out a fact the answer turns on (issuer or nonissuer, engagement type, framework, dates, amounts, materiality benchmark).
3. Would the answer differ under AU-C and AS, and does the stem say which applies? Flag it if not.
4. Would the answer differ under a standard that is superseded, or one taking effect in 2026 or 2027? Check the effective date in the source and flag it.
5. Any factual error in the explanation, including side facts and arithmetic?
6. Does the cited paragraph exist and say what the question relies on? Is the subject inside the blueprint's References? Is "group" right for the area?

Verify against the standards PDFs in the source folder your task names (read them with `pdftotext`; cite by paragraph number), PCAOB standards and rules (pcaobus.org), 17 CFR 210.2-01, 2 CFR 200 and 29 CFR 2509.75-9 (law.cornell.edu/cfr), 15 U.S.C. 7241 to 7266 (law.cornell.edu/uscode), and the Yellow Book GAO-24-106786 (gao.gov). Do not accept prep-course blogs, firm summaries or Wikipedia. If a source will not load and is not in the folder, list the question under UNVERIFIED; don't guess.

Do NOT edit any file. Reply with:
- PROBLEMS: each problem question id, what is wrong, the correct rule, the source and paragraph, and a suggested fix (corrected key, reworded stem, or "drop").
- UNVERIFIED: ids you could not verify, with what you tried.
- COUNT: one line with how many were verified OK.
Keep it tight; skip questions that are fine.
