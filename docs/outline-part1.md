# Exams July 1, 2026 to Feb 28, 2027 test the IRC, regs, IRS forms, instructions and publications as amended through December 31, 2025. Unless stated otherwise, all questions relate to calendar year 2025. Ignore legislation, court decisions, or penalty amounts after Dec 31, 2025.
# Question formats: Direct question; Incomplete sentence; "All of the following EXCEPT". Four options, single best answer.

## P1-1 PRELIMINARY WORK AND TAXPAYER DATA (14 scored)
Preliminary work to prepare tax returns; use of prior years' returns for comparison, accuracy, carryovers; taxpayer personal information (DOB, marital status, dependents, IP PIN, state photo ID); residency status and/or citizenship (visas, green cards, resident alien vs nonresident alien, ITIN); filing requirements and due date; filing status; sources of worldwide taxable and non-taxable income; sources of exclusions and adjustments (foreign earned income exclusion, retirement plans, HSAs, alimony paid, health insurance, SE tax); sources of deductions (itemized, standard); qualifications for dependency; sources of credits (education, foreign tax, child and dependent care, credit for other dependents, child tax credit); tax payments and refundable credits; previous IRS correspondence; additional required returns (employment, gifts, international information returns, other information returns); special filing requirements (foreign income, presidentially declared disaster areas, injured spouse); foreign account and asset reporting (FBAR, Form 8938); kiddie tax; ACA requirements (household income, premium tax credit, household size).

## P1-2 INCOME AND ASSETS (17 scored)
Income: wages and other earnings; interest (taxable and non-taxable); dividends and distributions; personal property rental; gambling income and deductions (W-2G, documentation); cancellation of debt (1099-C, foreclosures, insolvency); U.S. citizen/resident with foreign income (treaties, Form 1116, 2555, 3520, 5471); other income (scholarships, barter, hobby income, alimony, non-taxable combat pay, unearned income, taxable recoveries, NOL, illegal income); constructive receipt; constructive dividends; passive income and loss; pass-through income (K-1, basis, QBI items); royalties; state/local refund and other recoveries; 1099-MISC, 1099-NEC, 1099-K reporting and corrections.
Retirement income: IRA basis (Form 8606); traditional vs Roth IRA distributions; distributions from qualified and non-qualified plans (rollovers, 1099-R, QCD); excess contributions and penalties; early distribution penalties and exceptions; prohibited transactions; conversions and recharacterization; RMDs; plan loans; Social Security and Railroad Retirement taxability; inherited retirement accounts; foreign pensions.
Property: sale or disposition including depreciation recapture and 1099-A; capital gains and losses (netting, short/long-term, mark-to-market, virtual currency); basis (purchased, gifted, inherited); basis after stock splits and stock dividends; publicly traded partnerships; sale of residence (IRC 121); installment sales (related parties); options (stock, commodity, ISO, ESPP); like-kind exchange; non-business bad debts; investor vs trader.
Adjustments: SE tax; retirement contribution limits and deductibility; HSAs; student loan interest, alimony, military moving expenses, write-in adjustments; self-employed health insurance.

## P1-3 DEDUCTIONS AND CREDITS (17 scored)
Itemized deductions and QBI: medical, dental, vision, long-term care; taxes (state and local, personal property, real estate); interest expense (mortgage, investment interest, tracing, points, indebtedness limits); charitable contributions (cash, non-cash, limits, documentation); nonbusiness casualty and theft losses; other itemized deductions; itemized deductions for Form 1040-NR; QBI deduction.
Credits: child and dependent care; child tax credit and credit for other dependents; education credits; foreign tax credit; EITC (paid preparer checklist, eligibility, disallowance); adoption credit (carryovers, limits, special needs); ACA premium tax credit; other credits (energy credits, saver's credit, etc.).

## P1-4 TAXATION (15 scored)
AMT and credit for prior year minimum tax; household employees; underpayment penalties and interest; SE tax; excess Social Security withholding; clergy; military; income in respect of a decedent; net investment income tax; additional Medicare tax; uncollected Social Security and Medicare tax; other taxes (e.g., first-time homebuyer credit repayment).

## P1-5 ADVISING THE INDIVIDUAL TAXPAYER (11 scored)
Reporting obligations (1099, bartering, cash); property sales (homes, stock, businesses, antiques, collectibles); education planning (lifetime learning credit, 529 plans); estate planning (gift vs inheritance, trusts, family partnerships, charitable giving, long-term care, life insurance); retirement planning (annuities, IRAs, employer plans, early retirement rules, RMDs, beneficiary ownership, QCDs); marriage and divorce (settlements, common-law, community property, alimony); items affecting future/past returns (carryovers, NOL, Schedule D, Form 8801, negative QBI carryover); injured spouse; innocent spouse; estimated tax and penalty avoidance; timing of income and expenses; character of transaction (capital gain vs ordinary rates); MFJ/MFS/HOH advantages (joint and several liability); claim for refund conditions (amended returns); penalty of perjury.

## P1-6 SPECIALIZED RETURNS FOR INDIVIDUALS (11 scored)
Estate tax: gross estate, taxable estate, unified credit; jointly held property; marital deduction, portability; life insurance, IRAs, retirement plans in the estate; filing requirements and due dates (Form 706, Form 1041).
Gift tax: gift-splitting; annual exclusion; unified credit; effect on estate tax (GST tax); filing requirements (Form 709).
International information reporting: filing requirements and due dates (FBAR, 8938, 8865, 5471, 3520); covered accounts; penalties (failure to file, underreporting, statute of limitations); FBAR vs Form 8938 distinctions.
