# Brief for independently fact-checking new CPA TCP questions

You did not write these questions. Check them as a skeptical reviewer. The bank is the main study source for an accountant preparing for the CPA exam's Tax Compliance and Planning (TCP) section, so a wrong key teaches a wrong rule.

Scope: the TCP section of the AICPA's 2026 blueprint (/home/claude/tcp-blueprint.txt). The bank keys tax year 2025 law as amended through December 31, 2025, including P.L. 119-21 (signed July 4, 2025).

For EVERY question in your assigned file:
1. Is the keyed answer ("answer" is a 0-based index into "choices") correct for 2025 law? Recompute every calculation.
2. Is exactly one choice defensibly correct? Flag any question where a careful candidate could defend a second choice, or where the stem leaves out a fact the answer turns on (entity type, dates, basis, fair market value, holding period, elections, the applicable federal rate).
3. Does the key depend on recalling an inflation-indexed amount the stem doesn't give? The blueprint says candidates are not tested on those. Flag it.
4. Would the answer differ under law effective in 2026? Flag it.
5. Any factual error in the explanation, including side facts and arithmetic?
6. Is the cited source a primary source that says what the question relies on, and is "group" right for the blueprint area?

Verify against the Internal Revenue Code (law.cornell.edu/uscode or uscode.house.gov), Treasury Regulations (ecfr.gov) and 2025 IRS publications and form instructions (irs.gov). Do not accept prep-course blogs or Wikipedia.

Do NOT edit any file. Reply with:
- PROBLEMS: each problem question id, what is wrong, the correct rule or figure, the source URL, and a suggested fix (corrected key, reworded stem, or "drop").
- UNVERIFIED: ids you could not verify, with what you tried.
- COUNT: one line with how many were verified OK.
Keep it tight; skip questions that are fine.
