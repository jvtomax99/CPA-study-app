# PART 2: BUSINESSES

## P2-1 BUSINESS ENTITIES AND CONSIDERATIONS (30 scored)
Business entities: sole proprietorships; partnerships and qualified joint ventures; corporations; S corporations; LLCs; tax-exempt entities and associations; entity default classifications and elections; EIN; accounting periods (tax year); reporting requirements (W-2, W-4, 1099); hobby vs business and loss limits.
Partnerships: income, expenses, distributions, flow-through (SE income); family partnerships; partner's dealings with partnership (exchange of property, guaranteed payments); contribution of property and/or services (partnership's basis, property subject to debt); basis of partner's interest; disposition of partner's interest; formation (agreement, general vs limited partners, capital contributions); dissolution (sale, death of partner); filing requirements, due dates, penalties, audit notice requirements; partnership cancellation of debt; partnership-level audit (BBA) and opt-out.
Corporations: filing requirements, due dates, penalties; earnings and profits; shareholder dividends, distributions, recognition; special deductions and credits (DRD, charitable); liquidations and stock redemptions; accumulated earnings tax; estimated tax payments; corporate minimum tax credit; forming a corporation (services for stock, IRC 351, boot, property subject to debt); controlled groups; closely held corporations.
S corporations: qualifying requirements (shareholders); election procedure; income, expenses, separately stated items; distributions (AAA); shareholder basis (loan basis, distributions and losses in excess of basis, services for stock); revocation, termination, reinstatement; debt discharge.

## P2-2 BUSINESS TAX PREPARATION (37 scored)
Business income: gross receipts and other income; COGS (inventory practices, UNICAP); net income/loss and at-risk limits; cancellation of business debt.
Expenses, deductions, credits: compensation (deductibility, fringe benefits, family employment, statutory employee, reasonable); rent including self-rentals; depreciation, amortization (start-up, organizational costs), IRC 179, depletion, bonus depreciation, correcting errors; business bad debts; travel, meals, gifts; vehicles; interest expense (incl. 163(j) limit); insurance; taxes (assessments, penalties, sales tax, excise); employment taxes; casualties, thefts, condemnations; QBI (SSTB, calculations, phase-out, UBIA); general business credits (disabled access, R&D, small business health care, foreign tax credit); NOL deduction; home office.
Business assets: basis; disposition; like-kind exchange; converted property; capitalization and repair regulations (elections, de minimis safe harbor).
Analysis of financial records: business type and codes, year-to-year comparison; income statement; balance sheet; accounting methods and changes (cash, accrual, hybrid, Form 3115); depreciation recapture (1245, 1250, 280F); pass-through activity (K-1, separately stated items, nondeductible expenses); book-tax reconciliation (M-1, M-2, M-3); related party activity; loans to and from owners.
Advising the business taxpayer: reporting and filing obligations (extensions, penalties, international information returns, 1099 series, Form 8300); payment and deposit obligations (employment tax, excise); record-keeping (mileage log, accountable plans); entity selection; commingling; accounting method advice; transfers of property in or out; business life cycle; type of industry (SSTB owners); worker classification; timing, NOL, depreciation vs 179 vs bonus planning; ACA compliance (employer).

## P2-3 SPECIALIZED RETURNS AND TAXPAYERS (18 scored)
Trust and estate income tax: trust types (simple/complex, grantor, irrevocable, foreign); DNI and fiduciary accounting income; exclusions, exemptions, deductions; fraudulent trusts; income allocations, corpus vs income; separately stated items (K-1); filing requirements, tax years, penalties.
Exempt organizations: qualifying for and maintaining 501(c) status; applying (Form 1023, 1024, 1023-EZ); filing (990 series); UBTI.
Retirement plans: employer and employee contributions; reporting; plans for self-employed (SEP, SIMPLE, solo 401(k)); prohibited transactions; qualified vs nonqualified; nondiscrimination.
Farmers: farm income (livestock, crop insurance, subsidies, patronage dividends, conservation payments); farm depreciation; disaster provisions (drought, flood); farm rental (Form 4835); farm tax computation (Schedule J income averaging, Schedule SE, estimated tax).
Rental property: real estate professional; commercial vs residential; mixed-use/vacation home; passive loss limits ($25,000, MAGI); rental income (deposits, prepaid rent, not-for-profit); rental expenses (allocation, repair vs capitalize).

# PART 3: REPRESENTATION, PRACTICES AND PROCEDURES

## P3-1 PRACTICES AND PROCEDURES (26 scored)
Practice before the IRS: what it is; who may practice and extent; EA requirements; information to be furnished to the IRS; omission or error on return; former IRS employees and disbarred/suspended persons; advertising, solicitation, fee information; fees (contingent, unconscionable); due diligence; conflict of interest; refund check negotiation; standards for written advice, tax return positions and preparing returns; continuing education; enrollment cycle and renewal; prompt disposition; returning client records; PTIN; supervisory responsibilities.
Sanctionable acts: incompetence and disreputable conduct; OPR sanctions; frivolous submissions; fraudulent transactions (badges of fraud).
Rules and penalties: assessment and appeal of preparer penalties; penalty types (negligence, substantial understatement, overvaluation); furnishing return copy to taxpayer; signing returns and furnishing identifying numbers; keeping copies or lists; employees engaged during a return period (IRC 6060); preparer due diligence penalties.

## P3-2 REPRESENTATION BEFORE THE IRS (25 scored)
Power of attorney: purpose; signature authority (extension of assessment period, closing agreement); authority granted; limits on signing returns for a taxpayer; completing Form 2848; durable/alternate POAs; client privacy and consent to disclose (IRC 7216); 2848 vs 8821; changing, revoking, withdrawing representatives; CAF number; conference and practice requirements (Pub 216).
Building the case: identifying issues; criminal aspects; competence and time; conflicts; transcripts (e-services); taxpayer financial situation (ability to pay: installment agreements, OIC, CNC); financial health (bankruptcy, lawsuits, garnishments, insolvency); third-party research; discharge of tax in bankruptcy; IRS Collection Financial Standards.
Supporting documentation: financial records; legal documents; prior and later returns; contemporaneous documentation; entity documents.
Legal authority: IRC and regs; revenue rulings and procedures; case law; IRS forms, instructions, publications; private letter rulings; Internal Revenue Manual; authoritative vs non-authoritative; tax treaties.
Related issues: statute of limitations; post-filing notices (math error, underreporter); deadlines and timeliness (timely mailing rules); third-party correspondence; FOIA requests; tax avoidance vs evasion; disclosure statements (Form 8275); Taxpayer Advocate Service criteria; identity theft; judicial levels beyond EA representation.

## P3-3 SPECIFIC AREAS OF REPRESENTATION (20 scored)
Collection: extension of time to pay (Form 1127); installment agreements; offer in compromise; Collection Appeals Program; CDP (lien, levy, Form 12153); account adjustments (abatements, refund offsets); audit reconsideration; representing a decedent; notices and Notice of Federal Tax Lien; levy and seizure; Currently Not Collectible; collection summons; collection statute; trust fund recovery penalty; amended returns and refund claims (1040-X, 843, effect on statute); passport revocation.
Penalty and interest abatement: penalties subject to abatement (first-time abate, reasonable cause); interest abatement grounds; recalculation; procedures.
Audits/examinations: IRS authority to investigate; practitioner privilege (IRC 7525); verification and substantiation; time and place of examination; steps; RAR and 30-day letter; CP2000 and correspondence audits; taxpayer options (agree or appeal); burden of proof.
Appeals: right to appeal; request for appeals (protest elements); EA at appeals conference; settlement function; 90-day letter.

## P3-4 FILING PROCESS (14 scored)
Accuracy: reliance on software; miscalculations, duplicate entries.
Record maintenance: retention periods; data security (electronic, systems, paper).
Electronic filing: becoming an e-file provider (e-services, EFIN); e-file mandate and exceptions (Form 8948); advertising standards; ERO definition and responsibilities; levels of infractions (sanctions); compliance to stay in program; EFIN revocation appeal; e-file signature authorization and documentation (Form 8879, Form 8453); rejected returns and resolution (client notification, IP PIN).
